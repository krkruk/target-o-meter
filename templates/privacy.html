Cookie Policy

Target-o-meter uses a small number of strictly-necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. This page lists every cookie the site sets.

Strictly-necessary cookies

These cookies are required for login and security. They cannot be disabled — turning them off would break sign-in and form submission.

  • sessionid — the Django session cookie. It is marked HttpOnly and keeps you signed in across requests. It is set when you log in and cleared when you log out.
  • csrftoken — the CSRF protection cookie. It is read by the app's API client so authenticated form submissions and data-changing requests carry a forgery token.

Analytics & marketing cookies

There are no analytics or marketing cookies in use. Target-o-meter does not run tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or third-party analytics. If that ever changes, this page will be updated and the consent banner will re-prompt.

This is a technical description of the cookies in use; it is not a legal review.
